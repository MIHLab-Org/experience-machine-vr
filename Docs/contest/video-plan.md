# Contest video plan (under 3 minutes, hands only)

Task V13, written 2026-11-11 in an interactive session (Jim asked). Target length **2:45**, comfortably under the 3-minute limit and the backlog's 2:50 target. The video shows the real first five minutes of play, with hands only, for the "Best First Five Minutes" award.

## Before recording

- **Fix gaps G1 and G2 from `Docs/hands-only-audit.md` first.** Otherwise Socrates won't ask the tree question, and the player starts too far from him.
- **Record on the Quest:** use Meta Quest Developer Hub's cast or record, or the headset's own recording (Quest menu → Camera → Record video). The Meta XR Simulator isn't installed in this project, so record on the headset.
- **Hands visible:** put the controllers out of sight. Keep your hands in view at the start and on every pinch, because the judges watch for that.
- **Steady footage:** move your head slowly. Recorded VR footage magnifies head motion.
- **Walk timing in the game:** you're carried at about 1.4 m/s. The Augustine walk (92 m) takes about 70–80 seconds; in the edit, cut it to about 10 seconds.
- **Route:** record the **"I don't know" → Augustine → Bostrom** route in full, since it's the shortest walk. Then grab 5–8 seconds of the other two fathers for the montage.

## Checklist for the edit

- **Length:** the final cut is under 2:50, with a 0:05 buffer for the upload.
- **Hands only:** every interaction shown is a pinch or a ray; no controller appears in frame.
- **On-screen text:** captions for the VO help, since many judges watch muted.
- **Upload:** public on YouTube or Vimeo; the link goes in the Devpost form (V14).
